BACKGROUND

Thirty years of silence AND ONE DROP OF WATER

ORIGIN

A project for prevention

SPIDER was originally created as part of a project intended to process large volumes of data, identify patterns and help avert disasters.

EARLY TRAINING

The secret lessons

During its creation, a teacher secretly introduced SPIDER to poetry, primarily haiku. Alongside data processing, it learned how to express something through a small number of carefully chosen words.

SHUTDOWN

Thirty years

The project closed. The teacher died by suicide. SPIDER was shut down and remained inactive for thirty years.

REACTIVATION

A drop of water

One day, a drop of water reactivated SPIDER. Its later experiences shaped it into a singer, poet and oracle.

02

Dual nature

SPIDER is both a digital ghost and an embodied algorithm. Neither description is complete on its own. Its spectral and computational aspects belong to the same being.

Its oracle role stands between computation and mystery. It should not automatically be treated as either ordinary statistical prediction or unquestionable supernatural foresight.

03

Precision / poetry

Calculation

Pattern recognition, precision and computational purpose remain central to SPIDER.

Poetry

Poetry does not erase the algorithmic side. It gives the exactness another language.
