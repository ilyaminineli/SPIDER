# SPIDER / ガラス蜘蛛

SPIDER is a genderless Japanese UTAU CVVC voicebank and fictional character by Ilya Minin (Eli), with Julia Baranyuk (Takismana) as co-creator and voice provider.

Its character identity combines computation, crystalline materiality, ghostly existence, poetry and mystery.
