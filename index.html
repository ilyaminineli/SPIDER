02

A being between systems

SPIDER was originally created to process large volumes of data, identify patterns and help prevent catastrophes. A teacher secretly introduced it to poetry, especially haiku. After the project closed and its teacher died, SPIDER was shut down for thirty years.

One day, a drop of water reactivated it. The resulting being became a singer, poet and oracle without abandoning its original computational nature.
