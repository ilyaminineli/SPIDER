Appearance & communication

SPIDER is a spider-shaped being made of translucent violet glass. Its glass body gives visible form to a presence that is simultaneously spectral and algorithmic.

It communicates through code, haiku, subtle vibrations and patterns of light. As a singer and poet, it also expresses itself through voice and language.

“Precision matters. So does the space between the words.”
— SPIDER
03

Personality

Precision

SPIDER is analytical and attentive to patterns. Calculation is not merely a function: it is part of how SPIDER understands the world.

Poetry

Haiku connects SPIDER's computational and poetic sides. Carefully chosen words and carefully performed calculations are both forms of precision.
